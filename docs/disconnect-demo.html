What a disconnect looks like on the graph

ECE:4880 Lab 1 — two-sensor networked thermometer, 300-second chart recorder.

Illustration. These readings are made up to show the behaviour clearly. This is not a measurement and not live data — it is a fixed picture that never changes.

Last 300 seconds

Sensor 1 Sensor 2 no data

Before

Both probes reporting once a second. Two traces on a fixed 10–50 °C scale.

Disconnected — 10 s

Nothing is recorded. Both traces stop dead and the graph greys out for exactly the seconds that are missing, so the outage keeps its true width on the time axis.

After

Readings resume on their own. The traces start again on the far side of the gap — they are never joined across it, which would have invented data that was never measured.
